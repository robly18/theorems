All of the referee's proposed changes have been applied, and other changes were put into practice that I thought were in keeping with the spirit of the referee's feedback.

The following list summarizes the changes. Many changes are not mentioned in the list below. Changes not mentioned are minor changes of wording with no effect on content, for example changing words such as "shall" or "hence" to "will" or "then", as suggested by referee.

A number in parentheses, such as (7), indicates a change effected as a direct response to referee feedback. Theorem, Remark, etc. numbering refers to the new version.

---
Major changes:

(17) (18) In proof of Theorem 2.2.1, changed all mention of "workers" to "agents". The proof now uses the word "requirement" as the referee requested, but I was unable to find a clean way to state the requirements. Instead, I settled for using the language "requirement i is said to be injured when", where before I would write "injury occurs when". Referee seems to believe that I would not need to say what causes injury if I simply said what requirement i is, but due to the number of moving parts I sincerely believe that this would be impractical.

---
Minor changes:

(15) (20) Changed all quantifiers to follow referee's preference: Open parentheses, quantifier, variable, close parentheses.

Simplified exposition of axiom of transitive closure, defining it directly as V = union Valpha instead of defining it as "all sets are contained within a transitive set" and stating that this is equivalent to V = union Valpha.

Removed commentary on how PA def.eqv. to finite set theory is an interesting example. Instead I just state it.

(36) Restated Theorem 4.1.1 as suggested by referee.

(8) Removed commentary on how our two main definitions have, respectively, a CST flavor and a model-theoretic flavor. The definitions are straightforwardly stated with no comment.

(11) All warnings about the possibly non-standard usage of the word "atomic formula" have been relegated to the following sentence in the definition of atomic formula: "We do not consider the negation of a predicate to be atomic."

Removed explanation of how a 0'-computable c.e.-typed structure induces a structure in the classical sense.

Removed a remark highlighting how the application of Theorem 4.1.1 reverses the order of usual strong jump inversion proofs.

Removed discussion of how VV^c_1 and VV^c_2 formulas can be assigned indices. This is obvious, and anyway follows from the fact that we're working in the computable infinitary language, which already has that feature.

Removed comparison of VV^c_1 with Sigma^c_1 formulas.

Removed unnecessary text in Remark 2.1.5.

(28) Less detail on effectivization of Fact A from proof of Thm. 3.2.1.

(31) Shortened explanation of what makes the "effective method" actually effective into a single sentence.
